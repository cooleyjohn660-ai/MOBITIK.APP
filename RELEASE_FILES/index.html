01 Information We Collect

To provide our services, maintain the token ledger, and ensure security against multi-account fraud, MOBITIK collects the following categories of information:

A. Personal Identifiers (Google Authentication)

When you sign in using Google Sign-In (via Jetpack Credential Manager / Firebase Authentication), we collect:

  • Google Account Email Address: Used as your unique account credential and for essential service notifications.
  • Display Name: To personalize your dashboard and in-app profile.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed in your personal profile section.
  • Firebase Unique User Identifier (UID): An encrypted pseudonymous string linking your mining activity, balances, and referrals.

B. Financial & Web3 Wallet Data

MOBITIK provides internal digital ledger and cryptocurrency payout capabilities:

  • Internal Wallet Address: A generated address on the MOBITIK network used for internal token accounting.
  • Token Balances: MBTK mining tokens and USDT rewards accumulated through mining, swaps, and referral bonuses.
  • Destination Wallet Addresses: External public wallet addresses you enter when requesting USDT withdrawals (BNB Smart Chain BEP-20 or TRON TRC-20). We never request, collect, or store your private keys or 12-word seed phrases.
  • Transaction Ledger History: Timestamps, amounts, destination addresses, transaction status (PENDING, COMPLETED, CANCELLED), and on-chain transaction hashes.

C. Cloud Mining & In-App Activity Data

  • Mining Timestamps: Start time, 12-hour session duration, and claim timestamps.
  • Referral Network Data: Your generated referral code, who referred you, and the list of users who joined using your code.
  • Ad Interaction & Boost Counters: Daily rewarded ad counters (up to 5 daily boost sessions) and cooldown timer tracking.
  • Support Inquiries: Subject and message details submitted through the in-app support console.

D. Device & Diagnostic Information

  • Device model, operating system version, approximate country/region (derived from IP address for regional compliance), and Firebase App Check integrity verification tokens to prevent automated bot networks.

02 How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Service Operation: Managing 12-hour cloud mining sessions, calculating node reward distributions, and maintaining balance ledgers.
  • Authentication & Security: Authenticating user accounts via Google Identity Services and verifying that each human user operates a single account.
  • Payout Processing: Verifying, approving, and transmitting USDT cryptocurrency withdrawals to your requested destination addresses.
  • Ad-Supported Ecosystem Operations: Serving rewarded and interstitial video ads to fund network infrastructure and liquidity pool reserves.
  • Customer Support: Resolving support tickets, account queries, or transaction inquiries.
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting emulator spoofing, botting scripts, and terms-of-service violations.

03 Cloud Mining, Battery & Device Integrity

Zero On-Device Proof-of-Work: MOBITIK does not run proof-of-work mining algorithms on your physical device. Mining operations are simulated and hosted on remote cloud server nodes. The app does not overheat your phone, cause battery degradation, or consume continuous CPU/GPU processing power in the background.

This complies fully with Google Play Developer Program Policies regarding Device and Network Abuse and Cryptocurrency Mining on Android devices.

06 Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion and Data Retention Requirements:

How to Request Complete Account & Data Deletion

You have the fundamental right to permanently delete your account, personal data, and associated records at any time. You can request deletion through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Support Ticket: Navigate to Profile & Legal > Support Tickets, select "Account Deletion", and submit your request.
  2. Direct Email Request: Send an email from your registered Google account to cooleyjohn660@gmail.com with the subject line "MOBITIK Data Deletion Request". Please include your registered email address and username.

Data Deletion Process & Timelines

  • Verification: We verify the request originates from the authenticated account owner.
  • Deletion Execution: Within 30 days of receiving your request, all personal data, Google profile links, support tickets, and private database documents will be permanently purged from our Firebase databases.
  • On-Chain Note: Any transactions previously confirmed and broadcast to the public BNB Smart Chain or TRON blockchain are immutable by blockchain architecture and cannot be altered or removed from the decentralized ledger.

07 Children's Privacy

MOBITIK does not knowingly target, market to, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18 in jurisdictions where digital asset participation is restricted). If we become aware that a minor has created an account or submitted personal data, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated information. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided data can contact us immediately at cooleyjohn660@gmail.com.

08 Security of Your Data

We implement comprehensive industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security: All client-to-cloud communications are encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) protocols.
  • Zero-Trust Cloud Rules: Firebase Firestore security rules enforce strict per-user authorization, ensuring no user can read or modify another user's personal balance or private credentials.
  • No Plaintext PINs: Transaction security PINs are protected with one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hashing.
  • No Custody of Seed Phrases: We do not ask for or hold custody over external Web3 private keys or mnemonic recovery phrases.
